# The Craft of Professional Fountain Show Design (music-driven choreography) — notes as of 2026-10-20

Scope note: very little first-party "rulebook" material is published by WET, Crystal Fountains, Lumiartecnia, Aquatique or Chinese makers. Most hard detail comes from WET patents, Wikipedia, trade press (Pool & Spa Marketing 2021), vendor explainers (Forme d'Acqua, SafeRain, Huaxin, Huiqi), the OASE catalogue, and press quotes from WET choreographer Peter Kopik. Several sources were unreachable during research: CNN, Aqua Magazine, ResearchGate, ProSoundWeb (403 or 451 errors) and WaterShapes (connection refused). Vendor-blog numbers are marked as such.

## 1. How pros structure a 3–5 minute show (arc, hero moments, change rate, rest/darkness)

### Takeaway
Pros start from the music. They pick songs with strong dynamic contrast, cut the song into labelled sections (intro / verse / chorus / bridge / finale), and give each section its own "scene". The biggest nozzles (super- and extreme shooters, high central jets, flame) are held back for climaxes. Shows are kept to about 3–5 minutes because audience novelty fades after about 3–4 minutes. I found no published, numeric "percent of fountain active" rule.

### Cited Findings
- **Song choice:** pick songs with dynamics, "crescendos – both progressive and sudden". A song with flat dynamics only works as background. Label sections A/B/C (introduction, verse, chorus, final, plus bridge/coda/solos), and each section becomes a "sequence or pattern". — [Forme d'Acqua](https://formedacqua.com/en/innovation-and-technology/the-programming-of-musical-dancing-fountains/)
- **Contrast in the music gives room to work:** pieces with variation in intensity, strategic pauses and tempo changes give more room for dynamic sequences. Each scene defines jet selection, height, lighting and transitions. — [SafeRain blog](https://saferain.com/en/blog/how-to-make-a-dancing-fountain/)
- **Five-part arc:** the show is structured as opening, build-up, climax, transition and ending. "High central jets" are used for climax points. Flame is used for "dramatic sections, transitions, and final climaxes". Air explosions are used "selectively for impact". — [Huaxin Fountain](https://huaxinfountain.com/musical-fountain/)
- **Length:** the Hangzhou West Lake music fountain cut its pieces to keep each under about 5 minutes. Organisers said audience freshness lasts about 3–4 minutes. "Butterfly Lovers" (梁祝) was edited down to intro + lively climax section + coda = 5 min. — [Hangzhou News 2016](https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/chengshi/content/2016-04/12/content_6127566_2.htm)
- **Bellagio hero hardware is rate-limited:** each super-shooter is a 12-ft, 12-in-diameter water tank fed by a 200 psi, 60-gallon air receiver. It must refill after each shot, "so they can only fire so frequently". Each shot gives a loud crack. — [phcppros / Bellagio engineering](https://www.phcppros.com/articles/2835-an-engineering-marvel); [Wikipedia: Fountains of Bellagio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio)
- **Bellagio nozzle tiers (1,214 nozzles in total):** 208 oarsmen at about 77 ft (each rotates 360°, 140 gpm); 798 mini-shooters at about 100 ft; 192 super-shooters at about 240 ft; 16 extreme shooters at about 460 ft (added 2005). Water is driven by compressed air at up to 500 psi. Shows are cancelled at 25 mph wind. — [Wikipedia: Fountains of Bellagio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio). Counts vary between sources (for example 244 oarsmen, 250 ft) — [LV Advisor](https://www.lasvegasadvisor.com/amp/question/bellagio-fountains-nozzles/)
- **Dubai Fountain:** extreme shooters reach about 500 ft but are "used least" because they take a long time to build pressure (uncited claim on Wikipedia). The layout is five circles plus two arcs, 275 m long, with more than 6,600 lights and 25 colour projectors. Up to 83,000 L of water is in the air at once. — [Wikipedia: Dubai Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Fountain)
- **Kopik on energy pacing (Game of Thrones show, Bellagio 2019):** "We have to make sure that the fountain gives the same kind of energy, the same kind of drama, that people are associating this music with." The hard part was a score that is "continuously energetic... up and loud", which had to stay exciting without exhausting viewers. — [AP via Washington Times 2019](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2019/apr/1/game-of-thrones-takes-over-bellagio-fountains-in-l/)
- **Kopik on cultural beat feel:** "We have to get familiar with that particular culture and how the people... relate to music, relate to dance." For Arabic music, the principle is that the music is downbeat rather than upbeat: "you are actually bending your knee at the beat, not jumping up as in Western culture." He also rejects music that is "cheesy" or would "degrade the seriousness of the fountain". — quotes as surfaced from [Wikipedia: Dubai Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_fountain) and The National (2018), reported via search; the [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/travel/article/dubai-fountain-story) original returned HTTP 451
- **Aria/CityCenter (WET):** shows run seamlessly into one another. Wind sensors switch between "high" and "low" versions of shows. — [Las Vegas Sun 2009](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2009/dec/16/water-swirling-spewing-frozen-entertain-visitors-c/)
- **Avoid disjointed shows:** older systems looked like a string of disconnected pre-programmed sequences. Modern tools blend effects so the show reads as continuous. — [SafeRain](https://saferain.com/en/blog/how-to-make-a-dancing-fountain/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- An encodable arc would be Intro (low, partial, ambient) → Build (more groups, rising height) → Climax (full basin, tallest jets, flame, brightest light) → Transition/Release → Finale (hero shot), with sections taken from the song's own structure.
- Hero devices (shooters, extreme shooters, flame) should have a minimum re-fire interval (refill time) and a per-show budget. Using them rarely is part of what makes them "hero" moments.
- Kopik's downbeat remark means the accent placement and the "direction" of the gesture (drop or bend on the beat versus rise on the beat) should be a style parameter that depends on culture or genre.

### Gaps
- No source gave numbers for look-change frequency, the percentage of nozzles active per section, or how long dark or rest moments should be. Even the Bellagio/Dubai blackout and rest practice is undocumented in what I could reach.
- I found no published structure rules from Crystal Fountains, Lumiartecnia, Aquatique, Fountain People or Beijing Water Show, and no specifics on the Bellagio "Con te partirò" choreography.

## 2. Nozzle families → musical roles

### Takeaway
There is a consistent mapping across vendors. Moving or robotic nozzles carry melody and lyrical lines. Fast vertical (staccato) jets and jumping jets carry rhythm and bass. Shooters, high central jets, flame and air bursts are accents and climaxes. Mist/atomisers smooth transitions. Crown/central nozzles act as the "lead singer". Roles should rotate between songs.

### Cited Findings
- Moving robotic jets capture "the lyrical and melodic movements of a song". Staccato vertical jets "support tempo and highlight exciting sequences". Arcs and rings add visual richness. — vendor guidance surfaced in search ([Forme d'Acqua](https://formedacqua.com/en/innovation-and-technology/the-programming-of-musical-dancing-fountains/) / [Symphony Fountains](https://symphonyfountains.com/understanding-dancing-fountains-design-tips-trends/))
- Orchestra metaphor: size the "ensemble" to the fountain (large = symphony, medium = chamber, small = rock band). "The Crown Nozzle becomes the singer, the first round of nozzles... the guitarist, the second round... the drummer and the Jumping Jets follow the rhythms of the bass." "In the next song the roles are reversed." — [Forme d'Acqua](https://formedacqua.com/en/innovation-and-technology/the-programming-of-musical-dancing-fountains/)
- SafeRain mapping: crystalline (laminar-type) jets suit precise synchronised movement. Dynamic effects are for high-intensity moments. Atomisations smooth rhythm changes and scene transitions. Spherical jets "mark specific moments". Foaming jets add volume and stay readable from far away (focal points). One solenoid valve per effect is the most common route to individual control. VFDs vary height for smoother sequences. — [SafeRain](https://saferain.com/en/blog/how-to-make-a-dancing-fountain/)
- Huaxin mapping: chasing = directional rhythm following tempo. Swing = stronger sections or emotional moments. Variable-height jets follow intensity. High central jets = climax. Flame = drama, transitions, final climaxes. Air explosion = selective impact. — [Huaxin](https://huaxinfountain.com/musical-fountain/)
- Fountain People install (2021): 52 vertical chasing nozzles make "low arcs of water that meander throughout the fountain". Each nozzle can be shot up or down, popped or cut off. 6 robotic nozzles move side to side and front to back and twist through 360°. Chase scenes are choreographed so the water seems to follow the sound. Fog adds atmosphere. Choreography is done "beat-by-beat" and synced to "crescendos". — [Pool & Spa Marketing 2021](https://www.poolspamarketing.com/trade/features/show-fountains-attract-visitors-choreographed-music-water-lights/2/)
- Bellagio: oarsmen (360° rotation, about 77 ft) are the moving or "dancing" family. Shooter tiers at 100/240/460 ft are the accent and hero families. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio). Dubai: "oarsmen and water robots make the water appear to dance". Shooters give a "boom". — [Wikipedia: Dubai Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Fountain)
- Lumiartecnia's Digital Water Curtain is a computer-controlled "water plotter" that draws graphics and text by switching fast-acting valves. — [Lumiartecnia via ArchiExpo](https://pdf.archiexpo.com/pdf/lumiartecnia-internacional/how-we-work/80362-316693.html)
- WET's pixelated-display patent names nozzle categories: Bloom, Mini Shooter, Hyper Shooter, Analog, Sprite. Shooter-type nozzles cycle refilled → firing → refilling, with height set by pixel brightness. Continuous nozzles cycle startup → looping → dying → dead. — [US11813629B2, WET Inc.](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11813629B2/en)

### Inferences
- A role table a generator could use:

| Role | Families |
|---|---|
| Melody / vocal | moving 2D/3D nozzles, oarsmen, swing, crown or central |
| Rhythm / drums | vertical chasers, rings, staccato on/off |
| Bass | jumping jets, VFD pulses on large groups |
| Accent / onset | shooters, air bursts |
| Climax | high central, super-shooters, flame, full white light |
| Transition / ambient | mist, fog, atomisers |
| Imagery / narrative | water screens, curtains |

- Rotate role assignments between songs so shows don't look alike.
- Shooters and continuous nozzles need different envelope models: shooters are one-shot with a refill, continuous nozzles have attack/sustain/release.

### Gaps
- No primary-source guidance was found for lasers, or for water-screen projection timing within the music.

## 3. Layering, symmetry, call-and-response, spatial movement

### Takeaway
Explicit published rules are thin. Sources support grouping nozzles into concentric "rounds" and rings with separate musical roles, chases that "meander", arcs, lines and circles as group geometry, and attention to the main visual axes and height proportions.

### Cited Findings
- Combine effects with attention to how they relate to each other, "the main visual axes", and jet height and proportion. — [SafeRain](https://saferain.com/en/blog/how-to-make-a-dancing-fountain/)
- Jets "sway, chase each other, jump and twirl", like a dance. Separate rounds or rings play separate instruments. — [Forme d'Acqua](https://formedacqua.com/en/innovation-and-technology/the-programming-of-musical-dancing-fountains/)
- Show-control software lets nozzles be arranged "in circles, arcs or lines", with groups assigned to pumps and adjustable ramp up/down times. — [MediaMation](https://www.mediamation.com/?p=4984) (search-surfaced)
- Dubai geometry is five circles plus two arcs. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Fountain). The Yas Island (Crystal) fountain arranges nozzles and LEDs in a racetrack-like geometric pattern for choreographed sequences. — [Gulf Construction](https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/13110/Crystal_shines_with_Yas_spectacular)
- WET maps every nozzle to a 2D UV image and animates "wave patterns" as textures (pixel 0–255 = nozzle state). In effect, spatial movement across the basin is authored as moving images. — [US11813629B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11813629B2/en)

### Inferences
- Encode layers as foreground/low (chasers, rings), mid (moving nozzles) and background/high (shooters, central). Practitioner descriptions suggest 2–3 active layers at once, with all layers active only at the climax. This is an inference, not a sourced number.
- Spatial motion (wipes, waves, radial pulses) can be generated as 2D textures over nozzle UV positions, following WET's patent approach. Mirror symmetry is the default. Asymmetry and call-and-response between areas suit verse and dialogue passages. This is also an inference.

### Gaps
- I found no source with numeric rules for symmetry or asymmetry ratios, the number of simultaneous layers, or call-and-response timing.

## 4. Lighting and colour scripts

### Takeaway
Fountain sources say only that colour and intensity changes must be locked to the water and music, and that lighting is part of the choreography, not decoration. The concert-lighting craft adds more detail: each song has a primary colour, sections repeat with 4–5 looks, the climax is the brightest point, and white flashes are added as a section builds. Bellagio deliberately uses only white light.

### Cited Findings
- "Color transitions, intensity modulations, and water movements are executed with perfect synchronization." — [SafeRain](https://saferain.com/en/blog/how-to-make-a-dancing-fountain/)
- Lighting is planned with the fountain program, not as separate decoration. — [Huaxin](https://huaxinfountain.com/musical-fountain/)
- Bellagio has 4,792 lights. Steve Wynn chose white over coloured lights. — [Wikipedia: Fountains of Bellagio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio)
- Dubai has more than 6,600 lights and 25 colour projectors. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Fountain)
- Fountain People install: 60 RGB fixtures plus fog. Intensity and colour are adjusted alongside the water. DMX control. — [Pool & Spa Marketing 2021](https://www.poolspamarketing.com/trade/features/show-fountains-attract-visitors-choreographed-music-water-lights/2/)
- Concert lighting:
  - Find the song's statement and translate it into a primary colour.
  - Find the high point, which may not be the end.
  - Mapping repeating sections typically yields 4–5 looks.
  - Each cue should act as "musical punctuation not just flashing lights".
  - Source: [ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/pro-production-approaches-to-cueing-the-music-with-concert-lighting/2/) (search excerpt; page returned 403)
- The biggest chorus is lit as the brightest point. — [ProSoundWeb (song arc)](https://www.prosoundweb.com/story-telling-production-lighting-to-match-the-arc-of-a-song/). Example cue sheet: chorus alternates green and blue, with white flashes added as it builds. — [CUNY student deck](https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/amontesinos-eportfolio/files/2021/05/CulminationPowerpoint.pdf) (illustrative only)

### Inferences
- A rule set a generator could encode:
  - One base hue per song or section.
  - Hue changes only at section boundaries or downbeats.
  - Intensity follows loudness.
  - White or full intensity is reserved for the climax and hero shots.
  - Light drops toward dark before a hero hit for contrast.
  - About 4–5 distinct looks per song.
- The "dark before the hit" rule is inferred, not sourced for fountains.

### Gaps
- No fountain-specific source says whether light leads or follows the water. I found no published colour-per-mood palettes from WET or Crystal.

## 5. Programming workflow, tools, time

### Takeaway
High-end work is timeline-based: Syncronorm/Pangolin Depence for 3D previs plus programming, with export to controllers, or in-house tools such as WET's Maya-based pipeline. Mid-range work uses OASE WEPS, which is block-based with up to 1024 DMX channels. Output goes over DMX512/Art-Net/sACN, with SMPTE or audio sync and PLC handling pumps. Hand choreography costs roughly 2–12 hours per minute of show, and weeks per song at WET scale.

### Cited Findings
- **OASE catalogue:**
  - WEPS is freeware for "small to medium shows", with up to 1024 recordable DMX channels and "block visualisation". It handles pipe length and height differences, has a scheduler accurate to the second, and does live transmission to WECS III 512/1024.
  - For large shows OASE resells Depence (Syncronorm). Its fountain features include: timeline-based programming, programmer and palettes, timeline group tracks, node-based DMX routing, dependency connections (nozzle → valve → pump), a fountain height tool, real-time fluid simulation, fountain lighting and projection, show export to WECS, direct DMX output (Art-Net, ACN), a scheduler, a multi-user repository and node-based logic. Other modules cover lasers, stage, Special FX (flame, fireworks, CO2, fog) and Animate.
  - Source: [OASE catalogue PDF, "Show programming"](https://vandarkitekterne.eistrupweb.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Show-programmering-DMX.pdf); [OASE WEPS page](https://www.oase.com/en-US/fountain-technology/weps-software)
- **Depence R3 Fountain module:** real-time hydrodynamics, splash/collision sync, modular "snap-on" fixtures, and dependencies linking nozzles to valves and valves to pumps for hydraulic and lighting calculations. — [Pangolin](https://pangolin.com/products/depence-fountain-module)
- **Depence lets you program from a PC** without the fountain, which cuts programming time and cost. It offers 3D views, photorealistic renders and timecode-synchronised shows. — [Forme d'Acqua](https://formedacqua.com/en/innovation-and-technology/the-programming-of-musical-dancing-fountains/)
- **WET pipeline (patent, priority 2017, granted 2023):**
  - Import the layout (fbx/svg/dwg) into Maya with the "FieldAnim" MEL script and map nozzles into UV space.
  - Animate textures (0–255 per nozzle) at about 24 fps.
  - Assign nozzle categories through wizards.
  - Preview at "preview" or "final" quality.
  - The same data drives the hardware.
  - Source: [US11813629B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11813629B2/en)
- **Time and effort:**
  - Bellagio: up to a week for about 2 minutes of music. The first dozen songs took 2 months, with each nozzle and light programmed individually. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio)
  - Dubai: each new choreography reportedly takes 2–4 weeks. — search summary of [Dubai Fountain sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_fountain)
  - Grand Haven: about 2 hours of programming per show minute (older figure). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Haven_Musical_Fountain)
  - Vendor blog: 20–60 hours for a 5-minute show, and 100–200 hours for a 10-minute landmark show. — [Huiqi](https://www.huiqifountain.com/blogs/brand/dmx-fountain-control-system)
  - On-site programming can take "two to three weeks"; remote programming avoids this. — [Pool & Spa Marketing](https://www.poolspamarketing.com/trade/features/show-fountains-attract-visitors-choreographed-music-water-lights/2/)
- **Cue density and DMX numbers (vendor blog, treat as indicative):**
  - 500–2,000 cues in a 5-minute show (about 2–7 per second), and 5,000+ cues for a 10-minute show across 300+ devices.
  - Channels: valve = 1, RGB = 3, VFD = 1.
  - About 50 nozzles with RGB fit in one universe, and a universe refreshes at about 44 Hz.
  - Example pump ramp of 800 ms.
  - Workflow: import audio → mark beats and phrases → place cues → interpolate → simulate → upload and tune on site for wind, ambient light and viewing angle.
  - Source: [Huiqi](https://www.huiqifountain.com/blogs/brand/dmx-fountain-control-system)
- **Automatic choreography:**
  - Automatic choreography based on simple features (volume) is "seldom used" on real fountains because the results are "prosaic". Expert programming requires careful analysis of the music. — Yoo & Lee, "Creating Musical Fountain Shows", IEEE CG&A 29(5), 2009 ([Yonsei CGA](https://cga.yonsei.ac.kr/26fb79e7-b116-8195-a463-d4cece8f3352))
  - Wikipedia notes that most "automated" shows are still painstakingly hand-programmed. — [Wikipedia: Musical fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_fountain)
- **MIDI control:** a patent describes driving nozzle direction and valve flow from MIDI tracks, one track per fountain unit. — [WO2012150758A1](https://www.google.ca/patents/WO2012150758A1?cl=en)

### Inferences
- Pro tools converge on four things:
  1. A timeline with group tracks and section markers.
  2. An effect library or palettes (reusable "looks" or macros).
  3. A device model with dependencies (pump ramp and valve limits checked).
  4. 3D previs before export to DMX/Art-Net.
- A generator should emit editable timeline tracks per group, not a frozen stream, so humans can refine its output the way pros do.
- At 2–7 cues per second, a generator should place changes on beats and phrases but change whole looks much less often. 4–5 looks per song (Section 4) implies a look change roughly every 30–60 seconds, with beat-level accents inside each look. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of Crystal Fountains' or Lumiartecnia's in-house choreography software (I could not verify the "Choreo" name). Aquatique says only that it has a 30-person in-house team. — [Aquatic Show](https://www.aquatic-show.com/en/notre-societe/l-equipe)
- I could not access Chinese makers' (Beijing Water Show, Jiangsu/Zhonghao) software manuals. A Chinese-language search returned only general material.

## 6. Published philosophy, interviews, patents

### Takeaway
WET's public philosophy centres on matching the fountain's energy to what the music means to the audience, on cultural beat feel, and on rejecting music that cheapens the fountain. Its patents show image- and animation-based authoring.

### Cited Findings
- Kopik's quotes on energy, drama, culture and the Arabic downbeat (see Section 1). He has nearly three decades in fountain choreography and is WET's chief choreographer. — [Emaar / BusinessWire 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231123792143/en/Engineering-Wonder-Behind-the-Scenes-of-Emaars-New-Years-Eve-Extravaganza-at-Burj-Khalifa-and-Dubai-Fountain)
- A podcast interview exists: "Water Special Effects with Peter Kopik". — [Theatre Art Life podcast ep. 22](https://theatreartlifepodcast.podbean.com/e/episode-22-water-special-effects-with-peter-kopik/) (not reviewed)
- For the Game of Thrones show, WET worked with composer Ramin Djawadi on a custom score that follows the emotional beats. — [AP 2019](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2019/apr/1/game-of-thrones-takes-over-bellagio-fountains-in-l/)
- Bellagio's original choreography involved Kenny Ortega, with music by Gerard Schurmann. Its most popular song is "Time to Say Goodbye" (Con te partirò). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountains_of_Bellagio)
- Dubai reopened on 1 Oct 2025 with new lighting, sound and choreography. Phase 2 of the renovation is planned for 2026. — [The National 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/things-to-do/2025/10/01/dubai-fountain-to-reopen-today-with-new-lighting-sound-and-choreography/)

### Inferences
- "Water as performer" in practice means: give the fountain a dancer's vocabulary (sway, chase, jump, twirl, bend on the beat), cast nozzle families as instruments, and keep the biggest gestures in reserve.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full CNN Dubai feature, the WaterShapes articles, the Aqua Magazine show-control article, or the Yoo & Lee full text. These likely hold more craft detail. Treat the "2–4 weeks per Dubai choreography" figure as unverified against a primary source.
