## What was taken from the source package

The fountain design comes from `Desktop/Odaa_3Obsa_Show.aquapkg` with nothing changed: the engineering section, which holds the basin, all 1,816 fixtures with their settings, and the design groups. The new package also embeds the same song file.

The source package's patterns, light cues and cameras are not used. Everything in the show is new.

## The design

It is a 70 × 25 m D-shaped basin, with the audience on the straight front edge.

| Element | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ∞ infinity, 1D swing nozzles | 240 | 4.5 m jets on one path. Nozzle 1 sits at the centre crossing; 1–120 loop the right lobe and 121–240 the left lobe. Each nozzle swings along the path. |
| Portrait circle, 1D | 22 | 5.5 m jets on a 5.5 m radius. They swing in (dome) and out (crown). |
| Vario solenoid ring | 22 | 7 m jets on a 10.3 m radius. They can pop up to 20 times a second. |
| Sun rays | 130 | 3 m straight jets in 22 chevron rays around the portrait. |
| Pillars | 2 × 128 | Sword outlines inside the two lobes. |
| Front diagonal jets | 6 | Along the front edge. |
| Air cannons | 5 × 20 m left, 5 × 20 m right, 5 × 50 m centre | A circle of five cannons each. |
| Lights | 691 + 434 | 691 RGB lights paired with nozzles, plus 434 light-only fixtures that draw the Elder's face, the Odaa tree and a star. The portrait is kept green, as approved. |

**Lighting**
- Each underwater lamp burns only while its own jet runs, in the section's colour, so every water pattern is also a light pattern.
- Cannon lamps blast white.
- On the five biggest hits, every underwater lamp flashes white and the portrait flashes green.

**Cameras:** a film edit, with one moving shot per section, cuts on the music, and every key at least 3 m up.
- Call and response cuts to whichever side is playing.
- The crown is shot from a low angle looking up at the 50 m blast.
- The light dances are shot from above.
