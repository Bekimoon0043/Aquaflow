## Rendering decisions

- Keep the Old Man/Oddaa portrait green, as separately requested.
- Use density-aware accumulated pool light so dense RGB artwork does not become a white-green cloud.
- Keep lens/aura illumination independent of water so lights work with all jets off.
- Lower show exposure and bloom, retain hot cores, and scale accumulated light by pool area.
- Preserve distinct colors, individual lamps, water gaps, and the dark pool instead of maximizing brightness.
- Keep the original 1D crossing, scissors, fan, rowing, traveling-cross and crown sequences as editable recipes.
